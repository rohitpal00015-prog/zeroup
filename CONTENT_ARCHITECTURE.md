### 03 — THE PROBLEM
- **Headline**: "Content is not the problem. The system is."
- **Triptych Diagnostic Cards**:
  - `01 / IDEAS WITHOUT STRATEGY`: Brands publish constantly without knowing what they are building toward. High output, zero cumulative equity.
  - `02 / CONTENT WITHOUT DISTRIBUTION`: A good piece of content means little if nobody sees it. Creation without multi-surface routing is dead on arrival.
  - `03 / ATTENTION WITHOUT COMPOUNDING`: Views disappear. Systems learn. Rental models reset impressions every month; assets accumulate permanent audience.

### 05 — THE SYSTEM
- **Section Label**: `02 / THE SYSTEM`
- **Headline**: "From expertise to distribution."
- **Interactive 7-Stage Pipeline (Scroll & Click Synchronized)**:
  1. `01 RESEARCH`: Understand the audience before creating for it.
     *Inputs: Audience signals, competitor blindspots, search vectors, community queries.*
  2. `02 STRATEGY`: Decide what deserves to exist.
     *Outputs: Positioning, editorial pillars, recurring format briefs, cadence.*
  3. `03 STORY`: Turn information into something worth watching.
     *Outputs: Retention hooks, visual scripts, tension arcs, mental models.*
  4. `04 PRODUCTION`: Create efficiently without sacrificing quality.
     *Includes: Cinematography, high-density editing, motion graphics, AI pipelines.*
  5. `05 DISTRIBUTION`: One idea. Multiple surfaces.
     *Surfaces: YouTube long-form, Shorts/Reels, LinkedIn essays, newsletters, community.*
  6. `06 INTELLIGENCE`: Measure what people actually respond to.
     *Metrics: Second-by-second retention, repeat viewership, bookmark ratios, qualified inbound.*
  7. `07 ITERATION`: Every piece teaches the next one.
     *Loop: Data → Empirical Insight → New Thesis Hypothesis → Re-enters Research.*
